
# Chicken Tandoori Wrap

## Description

Chicken Tandoori Wrap is a flavorful Indian-inspired dish featuring tender chicken marinated in yogurt and aromatic spices, wrapped in a soft tortilla with fresh vegetables and a creamy yogurt sauce. It combines smoky, spicy, and refreshing flavors in a convenient meal.

### Ingredients:

- [ ] 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken breast
- [ ] 4 large flour tortillas
- [ ] 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt (for marinade)
- [ ] 2 tablespoons lemon juice (for marinade)
- [ ] 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
- [ ] 2 teaspoons tandoori masala
- [ ] 1 teaspoon paprika
- [ ] 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- [ ] 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- [ ] 1/2 teaspoon turmeric
- [ ] 1/2 teaspoon salt
- [ ] 1 cup shredded lettuce
- [ ] 1 medium tomato, sliced
- [ ] 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
- [ ] 1/2 cucumber, sliced
- [ ] 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt (for sauce)
- [ ] 1 tablespoon lemon juice (for sauce)
- [ ] 1 tablespoon chopped cilantro

### Steps for preparation:

1. Cut the chicken breast into thin strips or bite-sized pieces.

2. In a large bowl, combine 1/2 cup Greek yogurt, 2 tablespoons lemon juice, vegetable oil, tandoori masala, paprika, cumin, garlic powder, turmeric, and salt.

3. Add the chicken to the marinade and mix until every piece is evenly coated. Cover the bowl and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes.

4. While the chicken marinates, wash and prepare the vegetables. Shred the lettuce, slice the tomato and cucumber, and thinly slice the red onion.

5. Prepare the yogurt sauce by mixing 1/2 cup Greek yogurt, 1 tablespoon lemon juice, and chopped cilantro in a small bowl. Refrigerate until ready to use.

6. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the marinated chicken in a single layer and cook for approximately 10–15 minutes, turning occasionally, until browned and the internal temperature reaches 165°F (74°C).

7. Remove the cooked chicken from the skillet and let it rest for 2 minutes.

8. Warm each tortilla in a clean skillet over medium heat for approximately 20–30 seconds per side until soft and flexible.

9. Place a warm tortilla on a clean plate. Spread a spoonful of yogurt sauce across the center.

10. Add shredded lettuce, tomato, cucumber, red onion, and a portion of the cooked tandoori chicken.

11. Drizzle additional yogurt sauce over the filling. Fold the bottom of the tortilla upward, then fold in both sides and roll tightly to form a wrap.

12. Repeat with the remaining tortillas and ingredients. Slice each wrap in half and serve immediately.

### Notes:

- **Spice Level:** Adjust the amount of tandoori masala or add chili powder for extra heat.
- **Chicken Substitute:** Chicken thighs can be used instead of chicken breast for a juicier texture.
- **Healthier Option:** Use whole wheat tortillas and low-fat Greek yogurt for a lighter meal.
- **Cooking Tip:** For a smokier flavor, grill the chicken instead of cooking it in a skillet.
- **Meal Prep:** Marinate the chicken overnight for stronger flavor. Store cooked chicken separately from vegetables to prevent soggy wraps.
- **Storage:** Refrigerate leftover cooked chicken in an airtight container for up to 3–4 days.
